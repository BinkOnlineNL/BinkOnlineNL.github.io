GetIt – The Try to Click Game
Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

This policy explains how Bink Online handles information in the game GetIt – The Try to Click Game: the Android app (package nl.binkonline.getit) and the browser versions on Y8 and Playgama.

What stays on your device

Your best scores, recent runs (score, time and whether you played by touch or keyboard), credits, unlocked bomb skins, settings, the optional player name you choose and, in the app, your age range are stored on your device (in the browser, in that site's local storage). Bink Online does not receive this saved game and runs no game server or game account for it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage (or the site's data in your browser) removes it.

Leaderboards

In the Android app, if you use Google Play Games, your best score is sent to a Google Play Games leaderboard. Google shows your Play Games profile name and picture next to it, according to your Play Games settings, and processes this under its own policies. In the browser versions, your score is sent to the leaderboard of the site you play on (Y8 or Playgama), under that site's account and privacy rules; on Y8 the player name you chose in the game is shown with your score. Names are checked by a filter, but please don't use your real full name.

Advertising and Google's services

The Android app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform. Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Bink Online does not add a separate analytics SDK, sell data, or operate a gameplay-data server for this game.

Where required, the app asks for privacy choices before requesting ads. You can revisit them under Settings → Privacy options when that option is shown. Ads never change what you can play. Android also provides controls for your advertising identifier. Google controls the retention of information it processes; see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google may process information in countries other than your own under the safeguards described in its policies.

In the browser versions, ads are provided by the site you play on (Y8 or Playgama) under that site's own privacy policy and consent tools. The game itself does not add tracking.

Age range and advertising (Android app)

The app asks for a broad age range, not a date of birth. This choice stays on your device and can be changed in Settings → Age range. If you select under 16 or prefer not to say, the app does not request ads or start advertising consent collection. For ages 16–17, ad requests are marked as under the age of consent and limited to teen-rated ads. For adults, ads remain subject to the applicable privacy choices. The full game remains available regardless of this choice. Clearing the app's storage removes the age-range choice.

Audience

GetIt is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over, and is not directed to children under 13. Please contact us if you believe personal information from a child has been provided to us. The game does not request precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or payment information. There are no in-app purchases; credits are earned by playing and only unlock cosmetic bomb skins.

Support and your choices

If you email games@binkonline.nl, Bink Online receives your email address and whatever information you include so we can respond. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We keep support correspondence while needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. You can ask us to access, correct or delete information you have provided, or raise a privacy concern, using the same address. Applicable rights may also include restriction, objection and portability. You can complain to your local data-protection authority; in the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Deleting the local saved game does not delete information independently processed by Google, Y8 or Playgama. Their privacy controls and policies explain the requests and choices available for that information. No Bink Online game account needs to be deleted.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
